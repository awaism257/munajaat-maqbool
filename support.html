About

Munajaat Maqbool is the beloved collection of supplications compiled by Mawlana Ashraf Ali Thanawi (rahimahullah), arranged for the seven days of the week — presented in Arabic, Urdu, and a fresh plain-English translation, with footnotes and a reading-friendly design.

The Arabic and Urdu texts have been checked against the original printed booklet; the English follows the register of the ClearQuran translation.
