Privacy Policy

Munajaat Maqbool

Last updated: 7 September 2026

Overview

Munajaat Maqbool ("the app") is a free devotional app providing the supplications of Munajaat-e-Maqbool in Arabic with Urdu and English translations. It is distributed free of charge.

Data collection

The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. It works fully offline and does not connect to the internet.

What stays on your device

Your bookmarks, font-size choices, theme choice, and translation visibility settings are stored locally on your device only. They never leave your device and are deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

No analytics or tracking

The app and website do not use any analytics or tracking services. Nothing about your visit or usage is recorded or sent anywhere.

No accounts or ads

The app requires no sign-in, contains no advertisements, and does not use any tracking for advertising purposes.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, you can contact the developer using the contact email address shown on the app's Google Play listing.
