Munajaat Maqbool is a clean, modern, offline reader for Munajaat-e-Maqbool (The Accepted Whispers), the well-known collection of Qur'anic supplications compiled by Mawlana Ashraf Ali Thanawi, arranged as seven day-by-day sections from Sunday to Saturday.

Each of the 195 numbered supplications is presented in Arabic together with its Urdu translation (checked against the printed booklet) and a fresh, independent English translation made from the Arabic source, plus 200 source and instruction footnotes.

Features:

- Complete trilingual text: Arabic, Urdu and English, each language can be shown or hidden as you prefer
- Seven daily sections, 195 supplications in all
- Full-text search across all three languages
- Bookmarks for your favourite supplications
- Adjustable Arabic, Urdu and English text sizes
- Dark mode with a calm, reading-friendly design
- Works entirely offline: the app does not even request the internet permission

The app is free and open source (MIT licence). It contains no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no tracking of any kind. It is built as a non-commercial project; an optional Ko-fi link towards hosting and development costs is available on the project page.
